CTK 330 - Web Video

Context and Info

For my web video class, this was the first assignment that I did basically going through a day in my life. In the video, I tried to highlight some of the hobbies and things I liked to do in my free time, such as playing Sudoku and watching movies.

For this second assignment, the task we had was to edit a video using existing footage to make something new. Through this assignment, I experimented a lot with transitions, colors, and features to explore with audio in Davinci Resolve.

For my third assignment, our task was to create a story through mainly text with the help of images. No actual speech was allowed in the assignment, so it challenged me to figure out what parts of the story I wanted to keep or leave out.
